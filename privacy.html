Skylume — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

Skylume ("we", "us") operates the Instagram account @skylume.ai and uses the Meta / Instagram Graph API to publish content, respond to comments and messages, and read engagement insights for our own connected Instagram Business account and Facebook Page. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

1. Information we process

  • Content we publish: images, videos, and captions we create and post.
  • Engagement data: comments, mentions, and message threads on our own posts, and aggregate insights (reach, likes, saves, shares, follows) for our own account.
  • Interaction data: when you comment a keyword or message us, we process your Instagram username and message content to respond to you (including sending you a one-time reply with the resource you requested) and, if you choose to continue the conversation, to help with your enquiry.

We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we do not sell personal data.

2. How we use it

  • To publish and manage our own Instagram and Facebook Page content.
  • To reply to comments and messages you send us, and to deliver content you request.
  • To measure the performance of our own posts so we can improve what we publish.

3. Sharing

We use Meta Platforms (Instagram/Facebook) to provide the service. We do not share your data with other third parties except as required to operate the account or by law.

4. Data retention & deletion

We retain interaction data only as long as needed to respond to you and operate the account. To request access to or deletion of your data, email us at the address below and we will action it within 30 days.

5. Your choices

You can stop interacting at any time, unsend messages, or block the account. You may revoke permissions via your Instagram settings.
